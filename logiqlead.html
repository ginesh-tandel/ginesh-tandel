Case study 03 · B2B outreach · Multi-tenant SaaS

LogiqLead

Lead generation and email outreach where every team sends as itself, with tenant isolation enforced in the data layer.

ROLE
Architect & principal engineer
PRODUCT
Multi-tenant B2B SaaS
STACK
ASP.NET Core · Clean Architecture · Docker · PostgreSQL
LIVE
logiqlead.com
01

The problem

B2B teams need to find leads and run email outreach, but shared sending infrastructure means their mail doesn't come from their own identity.

Contacts, campaigns and replies are sensitive, so on a shared platform tenant separation can't depend on every query remembering a filter.

02

What I built

  1. 01

    Lead generation

    Tools to find and organise B2B leads for each team.

  2. 02

    Email outreach

    Campaigns sent from each tenant's own mailbox via per-tenant SMTP or OAuth.

  3. 03

    Tenant isolation in the data layer

    Separation enforced where data is accessed, not left to individual features.

  4. 04

    AI chat assistant

    A retrieval-grounded assistant that answers from the tenant's own data.

  5. 05

    Containerised deployment

    Runs in Docker on PostgreSQL with CI/CD.

03

Key decisions

Isolation at the data-access layer

One enforced rule beats hundreds of hand-written filters.

Per-tenant sending

SMTP/OAuth per tenant, so mail goes out under the customer's own identity.

Grounded AI only

The assistant answers from retrieved data rather than guessing.
