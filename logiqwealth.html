Case study 01 · Wealth management · AI extraction

LogiqWealth

Turning a pile of statements, insurance policies and tax documents into one searchable view of every client.

ROLE
Architect & principal engineer
FOR
US wealth-advisory (RIA) firms
STACK
ASP.NET Core · React · LLM extraction
LIVE
wealth.logiqcube.com
01

The problem

An advisor's knowledge of a client lives across bank and brokerage statements, insurance policies and tax returns, usually as PDFs. Pulling the facts out by hand is slow, and the picture is out of date as soon as it's assembled.

The firms already run their relationships in a CRM, so anything new has to fit into Salesforce or Redtail rather than replace them.

02

What I built

  1. 01

    AI document extraction

    LLMs read statements, insurance and tax documents and pull out structured client data.

  2. 02

    Client 360 profile

    Everything extracted lands in one searchable profile per client.

  3. 03

    Automated client reports

    Reports generate from the profile instead of being assembled by hand.

  4. 04

    CRM integrations

    Live connections to Salesforce and Redtail, so data flows where advisors already work.

  5. 05

    Meeting minutes add-on

    LogiqMoM records Meet, Zoom and Teams calls and writes AI minutes alongside the client record.

03

Key decisions

Provider-agnostic AI

Models can switch between Azure OpenAI, Gemini and Groq without touching business logic.

Role-based access

Financial data under role- and policy-based authorization, with architecture and data security owned end to end.

Built to run

Docker, CI/CD, automated tests, Sentry and uptime alerting.

04

Status
